# Implementation plan

Status: phases have not been derived. This outline is not an executable project plan.

Derive the sequence from this project's objective, user journeys, dependencies, risks
and completion boundary. Group coherent capabilities and put useful behavior and
important uncertainty early. Do not leave "implement the project" or generic
"scaffold / implement / test" steps as the plan.

For each actual phase, write:

- **Outcome:** the concrete capability this phase makes possible.
- **Readiness:** dependencies and evidence needed before starting.
- **Scope:** behavior/components changed and explicit exclusions.
- **Work:** implementation instructions, interfaces and failure cases.
- **Guidance:** relevant local paths/sections and why they apply.
- **Acceptance:** observable success conditions.
- **Verification:** real commands/scenarios, expected results and proof to retain.
- **State:** planned, active, blocked with reason, or completed with evidence.

Keep this the only active queue. A small project can use compact sections or a table.
When a phase passes its checks, reconcile progress with the objective and continue
ready work. Evidence can justify adding, combining, reordering or reopening necessary
work within scope. Phase completion does not require routine human approval.

Keep historical completion evidence here or in the named validation/completion owner.
Do not erase previous results when requirements change; record what needs revalidation.
