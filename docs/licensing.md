selected by the owner on 2026-09-30. [NOTICE](../NOTICE) identifies the copyright holder.
This supersedes the earlier, uncommitted MIT selection. The license is permissive and
includes the contributor patent grant in section 3; its scope is the necessarily infringed
claims described there, not a blanket grant over every contributor patent.

| Material | Applicable terms |
| --- | --- |
| Noetloom source and documentation | Apache-2.0 |
| Third-party dependencies and incorporated material | Their own licenses and required notices |
| Datasets | Explicit dataset-specific licensing, rights, and provenance |
| Separately distributed trained weights and checkpoints | An explicit artifact license selected before distribution |
| Noetloom name, logo, and branding | Separate trademark/branding policy; the software license grants no general trademark permission |
| Potential inventions | Patent strategy is a separate decision; contributions remain subject to Apache-2.0's patent grant |

An intentionally submitted contribution is governed by section 5 unless explicitly stated
otherwise. Do not import a separate contributor agreement from another project by implication.
Reference papers and sibling-project citations do not relicense their code, data, or weights.
Record licenses in artifact manifests before downloading or publishing research material.

The authoritative terms are the [Apache Software Foundation's license text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
